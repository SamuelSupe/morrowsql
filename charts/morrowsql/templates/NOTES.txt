Wait for three online MorrowSQL members:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get innodbcluster {{ .Release.Name }} -w

Use the Router service {{ .Release.Name }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local.
The cluster is a single-primary database. Clients must reconnect after failover.

Uninstalling the release retains database PVCs. Delete PVCs only when you intend
to permanently remove their data. This chart supports fresh installation only.
